# Privacy Policy — Guardias

**Last updated:** August 11, 2026

Guardias (“the App”) is a mobile application that helps you track on-call medical rotation schedules (guardias). This Privacy Policy explains what information the App handles and how.

## Summary

Guardias is **offline-first**. Your schedule data stays on your device. The App does **not** require an account, does **not** sync to a server we operate, and does **not** include advertising or analytics SDKs in version 1.0.

## Information stored on your device

The App stores the following locally on your device (for example, using on-device storage):

- Rotation periods you create (labels, pattern, dates, optional vacation ranges, shift start time)
- App preferences (such as language override)

This data never leaves your device unless **you** choose to back up or share your device through your own tools (for example, OS backup, screenshots, or future features you explicitly use).

## Information accessed on your device

- **Locale / language:** The App may read your device locale to choose a default language (English or Spanish). You can override the language in Settings.

The App does not request access to your contacts, camera, microphone, location, health data, or photos in version 1.0.

## No account or cloud sync (v1.0)

There is no sign-in and no Guardias backend. We do not collect, transmit, or store your rotation data on our servers.

## Third parties

Google Play (or other app stores) may collect information according to their own policies when you download or update the App. That collection is governed by the store’s privacy policy, not this one.

## Children’s privacy

The App is intended for adult medical professionals and residents. It is not directed at children under 13.

## Data deletion

Because data is stored only on your device, you can remove it by clearing the App’s storage or uninstalling the App.

## Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy when the App’s behavior changes (for example, if cloud sync or analytics are added later). The “Last updated” date at the top will change when we do.

## Contact

Questions about this policy: use the contact email listed on the Guardias Google Play listing, or visit [alanmatias.com](https://alanmatias.com).
