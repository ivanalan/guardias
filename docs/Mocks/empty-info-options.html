Notes (pros / cons) — no recommended pick yet

  • A baseline — pro: cheapest, clear copy · con: doesn’t show what share or widgets look like
  • B1 stacked cards — pro: faithful to real share sheet + both widgets · con: tall; may feel dense under a big title on small phones
  • B2 split panels — pro: equal weight for share and widgets; scannable · con: each side is smaller; widgets less “home-screen” glamorous
  • B3 hero + secondary — pro: best widget showcase; wallpaper stage reads instantly · con: share becomes secondary; link story is thinner
  • C1 share sheet — pro: teaches the real in-app share UI · con: less emotional “why share” than the handoff story
  • C2 link handoff — pro: clearest outcome (prefilled rotation) · con: invents a two-phone scene not in the product UI
  • C3 home grid — pro: strongest “lives on my home screen” feeling · con: doesn’t explain how to add the widget
  • C4 size picker — pro: shows both widget types + OS how-to hook · con: more didactic; less atmospheric than C3
  • Flow length — Flow 1 = 2 taps to CTA · Flow 2 = 3–4 taps. Longer only pays off if each step earns the visual.
